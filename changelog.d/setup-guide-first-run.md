### Added
- A setup guide on a fresh install: Ovis asks whether this is your first time, then walks through
  four short steps you can each skip: installing and signing in to a coding agent picked from the
  plan you already pay for (Claude, ChatGPT, or a free option), showing your plan's usage, using
  Ovis on your phone, and alerts. It ends by opening your first project with that agent. It opens
  by itself only once, when herdr has no workspaces and Ovis has no saved settings or paired
  devices; "I've used Ovis before" goes straight to the app.
